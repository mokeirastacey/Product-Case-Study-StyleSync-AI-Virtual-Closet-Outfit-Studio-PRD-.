Product Requirements Document (PRD)
​1. Document Overview & Metadata
​Product Name: StyleSync: AI Virtual Closet & Real-Time Outfit Studio
​Author: Stacey Mokeira
​Status: Draft / Conceptual Phase
​Target Audience: Consumer tech users (B2C), fashion enthusiasts, and busy professionals looking for frictionless daily styling.
​2. Executive Summary & Problem Statement
​The Problem: People waste valuable time every morning standing in front of a full closet thinking "I have nothing to wear," or they buy online clothes without knowing how they will pair with items they already own. Physical trying-on is tedious, and traditional shopping apps lack real-time, personalized visualization.
​The Solution: An AI-powered mobile app where users upload a base photo of themselves and photos of their clothing items (or online shopping carts). The app allows users to select an aesthetic or occasion and instantly generates hyper-realistic images of them wearing the curated outfits—without needing to physically change.
​3. Product Goals & Success Metrics (KPIs)
​User Engagement: High daily or weekly active usage driven by the "Daily Outfit Suggestion" feature.
​Decision Velocity: Reduce the time it takes a user to choose an outfit from 20 minutes to under 2 minutes.
​Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
​Average session length and number of generated outfits per user session.
​Social sharing rate (percentage of users sharing generated outfit cards to Instagram/TikTok).
​4. User Personas
​Primary Persona ("The Busy Professional / Student"): Wants to look put-together for class, work, or social events quickly without spending mental energy matching clothes every morning.
​Secondary Persona ("The Creative Shopper"): Loves experimenting with different aesthetics ("90s Minimalist," "Quiet Luxury," "Y2K") using both their existing closet and prospective online purchases.
​5. Core User Stories & Functional Requirements
​Feature 1: Digital Closet & User Profiling
​User Story: As a user, I want to upload a full-body photo of myself and snap photos of my physical clothing items so the app can build my personal digital wardrobe.
​Acceptance Criteria:
​Onboarding flow prompts user to upload a neutral full-body photo (base avatar).
​Users can take or upload photos of clothes (shirts, pants, shoes, accessories); the app automatically removes backgrounds (background segmentation).
​Items are auto-tagged by category, color, and fabric type.
​Feature 2: Real-Time AI Outfit Generation Studio
​User Story: As a user, I want to select an occasion or aesthetic and have the AI generate a realistic image of me wearing a matching combination from my wardrobe.
​Acceptance Criteria:
​A "Studio" screen where users select constraints: Occasion (Work, Casual, Date Night) and Vibe (Minimalist, Streetwear, Chic).
​Generative AI pipeline takes the base user image and realistically drapes/renders the selected garments onto the user's body proportion, adjusting for lighting and fabric texture.
​The app generates 2–3 diverse outfit options within 10 seconds.
​Feature 3: The "Mix & Match" Canvas
​User Story: As a user, I want to swap out a single item (e.g., change sneakers to heels) and see the generated image update instantly.
​Acceptance Criteria:
​Interactive preview showing the outfit breakdown.
​One-tap replacement drawer allowing users to test alternative pieces from their digital closet.
​6. Out of Scope (Version 1)
​Direct e-commerce checkout integration with external retail stores (to keep V1 focused purely on styling and generation).
